How to create your QR codes

Each row below has the exact URL for that piece of equipment. To create scannable QR codes, use one of these free tools — they work on NHS networks:

Option 1 — qrcode-monkey.com (recommended)Go to qrcode-monkey.com, paste the URL, click Create QR Code, download as PNG. Free, no account needed.
Option 2 — Type the URL directlyStaff who prefer not to scan a QR code can type the URL directly into their phone browser. The URL column shows the full address.
Option 3 — Bookmark on ward tabletsOpen the URL on a ward tablet and bookmark it. Staff can access the checklist directly from the bookmark without scanning.
